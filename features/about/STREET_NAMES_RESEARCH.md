# Street names research — aviation heritage

Research date: 2026-08-11 (initial pass). Updated 2026-08-11 with personnel-based findings
supplied by the project owner, plus spot-verification of those findings.

## Question

Of the 47 streets listed in `StreetsWeCover.tsx`, how many can actually be traced to
Schofields' RAAF / Fleet Air Arm / RAN aviation history (an aircraft type, a base unit name,
or a person connected to the base)? This backs the "Local history" callout on the About
page (`app/about/page.tsx`), which references Nabthorpe Parade, Ranary Way, Nirimba Drive,
Vampire, Sabre and Kittyhawk as examples.

## Base history (verified, sourced)

- 1941–44: acquired by the Australian Government, built as a RAAF diversion/adjunct airfield.
- 18 Feb 1945: commissioned as **RN Air Station HMS Nabthorpe** (Royal Navy Fleet Air Arm,
  British Pacific Fleet support base).
- 15 Nov 1945: renamed **HMS Nabstock**.
- 9 Jun 1946: handed back to Australia, became **RAAF Schofields**.
- 1949–51: used as the Schofields Migrant Hostel.
- 1950–51: **Gordon Steege** (later Air Commodore) commanding officer of RAAF Station
  Schofields, before going on to command No. 77 Squadron in 1951.
- Evaluated for the RAN Aircraft Repair Yard from 1950; temporarily **HMAS Albatross II /
  RANARY Schofields** (Royal Australian Navy Air Repair Yard).
- 1 Apr 1953: commissioned as **HMAS Nirimba**, an RAN Fleet Air Arm training establishment;
  from Sep 1955 re-commissioned as the **RAN Apprentice Training Establishment (RANATE)**.
- Units based at RAAF Schofields over its life (per RAAF's own 1975 chronology, extracted
  from NAA Operations Record Books): No. 22 (City of Sydney) Squadron, **No. 30 (Target
  Towing) Squadron**, No. 37 Squadron, No. 75 Squadron, No. 78 Squadron, No. 78 Wing,
  No. 114 Mobile Fighter Control Unit, **No. 386 (Base) Squadron**, No. 486 Maintenance
  Squadron, No. 14 Fighter Sector.
- Decommissioned 25 Feb 1994. Site is now UWS Nirimba campus / the Akuna Vista development.

## Implication for the About page copy

The "Local history" callout in `StreetsWeCover.tsx` only claims "several street names... and
a wing of historic aircraft types," and cites Nabthorpe Parade, Ranary Way, Nirimba Drive,
Vampire, Sabre and Kittyhawk as examples — all six are in the confirmed list above (and are
the aircraft/unit-name category specifically, not the newer personnel-based finds), so the
current copy doesn't overclaim. It also doesn't state a count, so it stays accurate even
after this update raised the confirmed count from 24 to 31.
