# Product Vision

Akuna Vista Local Directory exists to become the trusted digital hub for neighbourhood communities.

Instead of information being scattered across WhatsApp conversations and Facebook groups, residents should be able to discover trusted local businesses, community recommendations, events and services from one searchable platform.

The long-term vision is to create a reusable platform that can support thousands of communities while preserving the local identity of each neighbourhood.

---

# Mission

Help neighbours discover trusted local businesses through community recommendations.

---

# Problem Statement

Today, valuable community knowledge is temporary.

Recommendations disappear into long WhatsApp conversations.

New residents repeatedly ask the same questions.

Small local businesses struggle to remain visible after their advertisement disappears in the chat history.

Community knowledge should become searchable rather than temporary.

# User Personas

## New Resident

"I've just moved into the area."

Goals

- Find electricians.
- Find plumbers.
- Find childcare.
- Discover local cafés.
- Learn about nearby services.

Pain Points

- Doesn't know anyone.
- Searches repeatedly.
- Receives inconsistent recommendations.

---

## Existing Resident

"I've lived here for years."

Goals

- Recommend trusted businesses.
- Quickly find previous recommendations.
- Discover new services.

Pain Points

- Searching WhatsApp history is difficult.
- Recommendations become outdated.

---

## Small Business Owner

"I serve customers within a few kilometres."

Goals

- Reach local residents.
- Showcase services.
- Build trust.
- Receive enquiries.

Pain Points

- Limited marketing budget.
- Short lifespan of social media posts.
- Hard to remain visible.

# Non-Goals (Version 1)

The MVP will NOT include:

- User accounts.
- Reviews.
- Ratings.
- Messaging.
- Online payments.
- Marketplace.
- Booking system.
- AI search.
- Business dashboard.
- Mobile application.

These features will be considered only after validating product-market fit.

# Product Constraints

Current budget should remain minimal.

The MVP should use static hosting.

Operational costs should remain close to zero until demand is validated.

The architecture should support migration to a database without significant UI changes.

# Product Decisions

Always optimise for:

Community value before revenue.

Long-term trust before short-term growth.

Simple solutions before complex features.

Maintainability before premature optimisation.

Accessibility before visual complexity.

---

# Definition of Success

Version 1 succeeds when:

Residents stop asking the same recommendation questions repeatedly because the answer already exists in the directory.

Businesses voluntarily request to be listed.

Community members regularly return to the platform.

The platform becomes the first place residents look for local recommendations.

# Future Vision

Akuna Vista is only the beginning.

The long-term vision is to create a reusable neighbourhood platform that empowers communities across Australia to preserve local knowledge, support small businesses and strengthen community connections.

Every engineering decision should support that future while keeping today's implementation simple.
